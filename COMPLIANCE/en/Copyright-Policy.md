# Copyright Compliance Policy

**As of:** 2026-09-25 · **Deutsch:** [Urheberrecht.md](../de/Urheberrecht.md)

Policy under Article 53(1)(c) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, in particular
to identify and comply with reservations of rights under Article 4(3) of
Directive (EU) 2019/790. Structured along the copyright chapter of the
General-Purpose AI Code of Practice. The German version is authoritative.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers all data used to calibrate or train Myelith artefacts,
third-party code and models in the project, and the system's outputs. The
provider is responsible (see [GPAI documentation](GPAI-Documentation.md)).

## 2. Training and calibration data

1. **No collection from the internet.** Myelith does not operate a
   crawler today. The data used is either generated by rules within the
   project or a public dataset under a free licence (see
   [training data](Training-Data.md)).
2. **Should web content be collected in future**, then only as follows:
   - `robots.txt` is read and obeyed, including user agents that exclude
     AI training.
   - Other machine-readable reservations (metadata, protocols such as
     TDMRep, terms of use where machine-readable) are recognised and
     obeyed.
   - No content behind paywalls or access barriers that would have to be
     circumvented.
   - No sources known for copyright infringement.
   - The crawler identifies itself with its own documented user agent.
3. **Every corpus for the network** goes through the corpus admission
   procedure: origin and legal basis of every component, evidence for
   every class of the exclusion catalogue, anchoring by Merkle root
   ([template](../ethics/vorlagen/korpus-aufnahmeantrag.json)).
4. **Fine-tuning by users** with their own material (book pipeline) takes
   place on the user's computer. Whether the user may use the material
   for this is their own decision and responsibility; the pipeline points
   this out.

## 3. Third-party models and code

- **Base models** are used only if their licence is Apache 2.0 or MIT
  (principle G7 of the [ethics manifesto](../ethics/Manifest.md)),
  checked per variant ([licence review](../ethics/Lizenzlage.md)) and in
  CI ([`lizenzprobe.py`](../ethics/werkzeuge/lizenzprobe.py)).
- **All third-party components** are listed with their licence in
  [NOTICES](../NOTICES.md), generated from the package metadata and
  checked against it in CI.
- **Third-party source code is not copied.** An idea from another project
  is read, understood and rewritten; lines carry a licence, an idea does
  not.

## 4. Outputs

- Language models may in rare cases remember and reproduce passages from
  their training data. Myelith does not fine-tune the base models on
  copyrighted works and thus does not increase this risk.
- **Content from web research** enters the answer as a cited source with
  its address; the model is instructed to summarise and refer rather than
  copy long passages verbatim.
- **Synthetic outputs** (voice) are marked as AI-generated (see
  [intended use policy](Intended-Use-Policy.md), section 3).
- The intended use policy prohibits using outputs to infringe copyright.
  Whoever uses an output is responsible for that use.

## 5. Complaints by rightholders

Anyone who believes Myelith infringes their rights reports this via the
repository's issues, naming the work concerned and where it appears.
Every report is examined and answered; justified objections lead to the
material being removed from future corpora and, where possible, to a
correction.

## 6. Review

This policy is reviewed whenever the data sources change and at least
once a year. The date is given at the top.
