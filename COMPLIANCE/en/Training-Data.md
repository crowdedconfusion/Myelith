# Summary of Training Content

**As of:** 2026-09-25 · **Deutsch:** [Trainingsdaten.md](../de/Trainingsdaten.md)

Public summary of the content used for training under Article 53(1)(d)
of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Structured along the template of the AI
Office: general information, data sources, data processing. The German
version is authoritative.

## 1. General information

| Field | Information |
|---|---|
| Provider | Joschka Benjamin Hänsler, private individual, Germany |
| Models | `myelith-0.6b`, `myelith-4b`, `myelith-8b`, `myelith-30b-a3b`, `myelith-35b-a3b` |
| Modalities of the training data | text |
| Size of own training data | about 127,000 words (synthetic), plus 241,000 words used for calibration only |
| Period of data collection | 2026-08 to 2026-09 |
| Base models | Qwen3 series and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (Alibaba Cloud); their training data is described by their provider |

⚑ **Two levels, and this summary concerns the second.** The base models
were pre-trained by their provider on a very large multilingual corpus;
Myelith has no knowledge of it beyond what that provider publishes. The
Myelith artefacts are derived from them by conversion, calibration and
own fine-tuning, and only that data is listed here.

## 2. Data sources

### 2.1 Publicly available datasets

| Dataset | Use | Size | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| WikiText-2 (`wikitext-2-raw-v1`, test split) | **Calibration** of activation scales and perplexity measurement; changes no weights | about 241,000 words | CC BY-SA 3.0 or GFDL |

### 2.2 Licensed private datasets

None.

### 2.3 Data crawled or scraped from the internet

**None.** Myelith operates no crawler and collects no training data from
the web. The assistant's web research reads pages only to answer a
user's question; what it reads is not used for training.

### 2.4 User data

**None that reaches the project.** Myelith runs on the user's computer.
Conversations, attachments and voice samples do not leave that computer
and are not used to train the published artefacts.

A user can fine-tune **their own** artefacts with their own material
(book pipeline in `TRAINING/korpus/`). This training takes place on the
user's computer; the user is responsible for the rights to the material
(see [copyright policy](Copyright-Policy.md)).

### 2.5 Synthetic data

| Dataset | Content | Size | Generator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reference ladder | five stages with a known rule (copy, final letter, reversal, two- and three-digit addition), 4,000 training and 800 hold-out lines each | 24,000 lines, about 120,000 words | `BENCHMARKS/Training/referenzleiter.py` |
| Knowledge seed | invented persons and an invented event with test questions | about 700 lines, about 6,700 words | `BENCHMARKS/Training/wissenssaat.py` |

Both are **generated by rules**, without a language model and without
third-party texts. They measure what a training step stores; the
knowledge seed is deliberately invented so that no already known
knowledge is measured. They are regenerated when needed and not
versioned.

### 2.6 Other sources

None.

## 3. Data processing

### 3.1 Respecting reservations of rights (text and data mining)

As Myelith collects no content from the internet for training, there are
no reservations under Art. 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 to respect.
Should the project use web content for training in future, the rules of
the [copyright policy](Copyright-Policy.md) apply (machine-readable
reservations, `robots.txt`).

### 3.2 Removal of illegal content

The own data is generated by rules or comes from an established public
dataset that is only used for calibration, not training. For future
corpora, the project's **corpus admission procedure** requires evidence
for every class of the exclusion catalogue
([`../ethics/Ausschluss.json`](../ethics/Ausschluss.json)) as well as the
origin and legal basis of every component
([template](../ethics/vorlagen/korpus-aufnahmeantrag.json)).

### 3.3 Quality assurance

- **Hold-out sets** with disjoint examples of the same rule; a training
  run only counts against them and against a noise baseline.
- **Provenance**: corpora for the network are anchored by a Merkle root
  (`TRAINING/myl-train`), so that every line can be traced to its source.

### 3.4 Known gaps and biases

- The own data is small and artificial; it does not broadly change the
  artefacts' knowledge or behaviour.
- Biases of the base models (languages, cultural areas, topics) are
  inherited and not corrected by Myelith.
- WikiText-2 is in English; calibration is therefore tuned to English
  text.
